A tiny Python program that writes one short rant to `/output/entry.txt`: at most 400 characters about a smoke detector whose low-battery chirp stays silent all day like a model citizen and hunts at 3 a.m., the moment your head touches the pillow. The rant was picked by drafting ten candidates and duelling them pairwise on the bounty judge's own criteria — a funny rant about one everyday annoyance, a real laugh rather than performed anger, a specific concrete scene, economical wording with no throat-clearing, and a fresh angle — then tightening the winner's scene until every sentence earned its place. The drafts and duels are kept in `candidates.md`.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```
